## Daily Study Loop

### Start — 10 minutes

- 5 questions from yesterday
- 1 question from an older weak topic
- Review yesterday's mistake log

### Learn — 45–90 minutes

- Diagram first
- Short explanation
- Service comparison
- Example

### Build — 30–60 minutes

Complete the day's hands-on task.

### Check — 15–25 minutes

- 5–10 exam-style questions
- Target ≥80%
- Explain *why* the selected Azure service is correct

### Close — 5 minutes

Record:

- Completed topics
- Quiz score
- Hands-on completed
- Confidence 1–5
- Weak areas
- Questions to revisit tomorrow

## Recovery Rule

If a day is missed:

1. Do **not** shift the whole schedule.
2. Move only unfinished MUST-KNOW items into the next buffer slot.
3. Skip optional content first.
4. Keep the final mock and final revision dates protected.
